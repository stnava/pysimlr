3. Review Findings & Non-Blocking Observations

[Minor] Top-Level Export Visibility for sparse_dist

sparse_dist is implemented in src/pysimlr/sparse.py, but is not imported into src/pysimlr/__init__.py. Direct import from pysimlr import sparse_dist raises ImportError, whereas from pysimlr.sparse import sparse_dist succeeds.

Recommendation: Export sparse_dist in src/pysimlr/__init__.py and add to __all__ in a future polish pass.

[Minor] Docstring Shape Nuance in multiscale_svd

multiscale_svd docstring documents evals_vs_scale as shape (len(r), nev), but when nev > min(knn, p), the returned tensor is bounded to (len(r), min(nev, max_rank)).

Recommendation: Update docstring to clarify that the second dimension is min(nev, max_rank).

[Info] PyTorch Learning Rate Scheduler Step Order

In src/pysimlr/flows.py:479, a UserWarning notes that lr_scheduler.step() is called before optimizer.step() during flow training.

Recommendation: Reorder to optimizer.step() then lr_scheduler.step() during Milestone 2 ANTsTorch flow refactoring.
