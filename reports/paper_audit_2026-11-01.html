Manuscript audit: paper/, 2026-11-01

pysimlr working tree (v0.3.0, uncommitted). Built with make paper (Quarto 1.9.36) from the existing caches in paper/results_cache; HTML and PDF written to paper/_book and copied to docs/manuscript. Only appendix_flow_simr_v.qmd re-executed; every other chapter rendered from its freeze cache.

Verdict

  • The paper's central comparative claim survives: multi-view models rank well ahead of concatenated PCA (Friedman χ² 31.5, p 1.1e-04 on the cache; 30.5, p 1.7e-04 when regenerated with v0.3.0).
  • About 25 numbers in the prose, abstract and captions do not match the caches they cite. Several change a stated conclusion, including the abstract's headline ADNI-MCI result.
  • Six result caches have no generating script in the repository, and the hyperparameter-sweep cache cannot be reproduced by the committed code.
  • The appendix Wilcoxon tests treat 36 dataset–seed pairs as independent. At the 12-dataset level, most "beats PCA" claims lose significance.

"recomputed here" means re-measured independently of the delegated audit; "agent" means recomputed by the delegated audit agent from the caches (its scripts: /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-stnava-data-repos-pysimlr/11c95b66-6559-41b1-8205-9fbf1574b41d/scratchpad/paper_audit/).
