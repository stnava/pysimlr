amigo: identified loadings for every pysimlr fit

Branch amigo. Evidence: docs/audit/evidence/amigo_loadings_*.csv, constraint_selection.csv, linear_tol_selection.csv. Script: scripts/amigo_loadings_evaluation.py. Tracking: docs/tracking_amigo.md.

Summary.
  • The encoder V is weakly identified: two refits on 98% of the rows move it substantially, while the latent U barely moves.
  • Every fit now also returns structure (feature-latent correlations, in a canonical varimax basis) and loadings (their OLEE summary). On synthetic data these are 50-150× more stable than V and meet the contract exactly: column coherence 0, and support overlap 0 under positivity.
  • On ADNI-MCI the loadings are only as stable as U. At k = 3 one component is not identified; at k = 2 under positivity everything is stable.

5. Known costs and open items

  • Three measured regressions from removing the defect penalty are recorded as strict xfails (thresholds unchanged): the near-Stiefel tolerance at nsa_w=1, the face-residual calibration, and one support-recovery fixture (0.978 → 0.813). See A3.
  • Positive-mode V is less stable without the penalty (0.030 → 0.161 on the planted case). The loadings are unaffected.
  • Plug-in structure is inflated because U is fitted on the same rows. Thresholds come from loading_inference, never from point estimates.
  • The paper still reads V and the removed Lobe_Crosstalk column (A2).
