4.1 Synthesizing Clinical Neurobiology and Statistical Representation Learning

The empirical results across ADNI-MCI, TCGA-BRCA, TCGA-KIRC, and the synthetic ground-truth benchmarks demonstrate that predictive accuracy and mechanistic localization do not need to be traded against one another when multi-view integration is organized around an explicit linear entrypoint (\(\mathbf{Z}_{m,\text{lin}} = \mathbf{X}_m \mathbf{V}_m\)). For the clinical neuroscientist or neuropsychologist, the primary value of SiMR lies in how cross-modal supervision shapes the learned anatomical basis. When structural MRI is decomposed in isolation via PCA, every one of the \(62\) DKT parcels loads on every axis, and the result explains \(R^2 =\) 0.152 of composite cognitive severity (CogPC1), falling to 0.044 under single-axis decision tree splits. Coupling structural MRI views to an 11-scale cognitive battery during training (Protocol B) with non-negative approximate Stiefel regularization (nsa_flow, \(w = 0.50, q = 0.85\)) concentrates the held-out prediction in one component (vol/area \(R^2 =\) 0.211 alone), whose replicated features are entorhinal, temporal-lobe and inferior parietal measures (Figure 3.9). Whether this pattern corresponds to stages of neurofibrillary pathology (Braak and Braak 1991; Dickerson et al. 2009; Jack et al. 2018) is not tested here. On DiReCT cortical thickness the same component tracks a global factor (58% of the variance), and its regional basis replicates only when the collapse penalty is turned off (collapse_weight = 0).

For the machine learning researcher, our four-probe evaluation resolves why post-hoc rotation or unmixing steps (ICA (Hyvärinen and Oja 2000), orthogonal Procrustes rotations (Schönemann 1966), or dense SVD factors) often appear competitive under ridge regression yet fail when individual coordinates are inspected or thresholded. Because \(\operatorname{span}(\mathbf{X}_m \mathbf{V}_m \mathbf{M}) = \operatorname{span}(\mathbf{X}_m \mathbf{V}_m)\) for any invertible \(\mathbf{M} \in \mathrm{GL}(k)\), linear subspace probes measure only aggregate subspace capacity. Pairing linear subspace probes with coordinate-aligned Random Forest probes (RF(XV)) and ground-truth support recovery (\(F_1\)) exposes diagonal coordinate mixing directly: across all twelve benchmarks, the Random Forest minus linear score is -0.044 for PCA, against NED (-0.014), SiMLR (-0.016) for the models with the smallest drop, and support recovery on the \(50\%\)-sparse Linear regime ranges from \(F_1 =\) 0.500 to 1.000.

4.3 Limitations and Negative Findings

Rigorous experimental reporting requires highlighting the regimes where SiMR architectures underperform, incur computational trade-offs, or require careful preprocessing:

  1. No Universal Advantage Over PCA on Unaligned Single-View Unsupervised Tasks: When a single homogeneous data matrix is analyzed without cross-modal supervision or when views share identical signal-to-noise ratios and dense Gaussian loadings, standard PCA maximizes variance explanation in \(\mathcal{O}(\min(N p^2, p N^2))\) time (median 0.011 s here). On low-dimensional clinical tables such as Heart (\(p = [7, 6]\)), NSAFlow-PCA reaches 0.848 against 0.819 for multi-view SiMLR.
  2. Unrotated Initialization and Row-Mean Centering on Cortical Thickness: On ADNI-MCI, non-negative SiMLR without rotation or quantile thresholding (varimax = False, sparseness = None) is 67% sparse with [60, 1, 1] active DKT regions per component; with Varimax rotation it is 67% sparse with [2, 0, 60]. Subject row-mean centering of the MRI views (Appendix B; the cognitive outcome is not row-centred) gives 59% sparsity and CogPC1 \(R^2 =\) 0.162 (PCA start) or 0.193 (ICA start), against 0.196 with column centering. Sparsity alone does not make the thickness basis interpretable: at \(q = 0.85\) the components keep [2, 60, 0] regions, because the collapse penalty reduces columns on a globally correlated view to one or two regions, and the regional pattern appears only with collapse_weight = 0 (Figure 3.9 D).
  3. Extreme Orthogonality Weights: Moving the nsa_flow weight from \(w = 0.50\) to \(w = 0.95\) multiplies fit time by 6.8 on ADNI-MCI and 3.3 on synthetic NonnegParts, and changes CogPC1 \(R^2\) from 0.215 to 0.216. At \(w = 0.01\) the effective rank is 2.98 of \(k = 3\) (Gram defect \(D =\) 0.010); at \(w = 0.10\) it is 3.00. Across \(w \in [0.20, 0.85]\), CogPC1 \(R^2\) stays within 0.201–0.218.
  4. Deep Multi-View Models and Feature Support Recovery: On the synthetic parts regimes, the deep autoencoder variants recover feature supports less well than the linear solver: \(F_1 =\) 0.752 (NED) and 0.713 (NEDPP) on NonnegParts against 0.579 for SiMLR, and 0.465 and 0.447 on NonnegPartsWeak against 0.644.
  5. Dimensional Collapse vs. Correlated Factor Trade-Off (collapse_threshold = 0.3): Preventing dimensional collapse is essential in narrow or unbalanced views. The default penalty (collapse_weight = 1.0, collapse_threshold = 0.3; docs/audit/evidence/collapse_threshold_selection.csv) protects narrow views from collapsing into duplicate factors (at \(t = 0.7\), narrow-view score correlation reaches \(0.89\) and outcome \(R^2\) drops to \(-0.10\)), at the trade-off of capping recoverable score correlation between correlated latent factors near \(t = 0.3\) under average mixing (avg: \(0.302\) at \(\rho = 0.5\) and \(0.309\) at \(\rho = 0.7\)), whereas Newton polar mixing decorrelates consensus scores to \(0.000\).
  6. Unified Budgets and Optimizer Convergence Basins: Optimization uses a unified default budget of \(300\) linear sweeps and \(300\) deep epochs with plateau tolerance DEFAULT_TOL = 1e-4 (docs/audit/evidence/default_selection_stage2.csv, docs/audit/evidence/deep_default_consistency.csv). Dimensional collapse in unpenalized linear and deep fits is a step-size optimization basin rather than an objective minimum: unpenalized deep fits that collapse incur worse similarity loss than uncollapsed fits (mean \(-1.48\), \(n = 7\), versus \(-1.94\), \(n = 13\); docs/audit/evidence/deep_collapse_diagnosis.csv), and short-horizon step-size probes favor upper grid endpoints, motivating the fixed probe grid [1.0, 0.001].
  7. Scale-Free View Weighting and Standardization Invariance: Equal view weights \(\alpha_m = 1/M\) preserve scale invariance for normalized correlation and related scale-free energies, preventing view-weight spreads from muting the unweighted collapse penalty, while standardized energies (align, logcosh, exp, kurtosis, negentropy) use a scale-relative variance floor so rescaling any view leaves the objective invariant.

4.4 Conclusion

By enforcing an explicit, sparse, non-negative, and approximately orthogonal linear projection at the input boundary of every modality, the SiMR framework bridges classical multivariate biostatistics and modern multi-modal representation learning. Clinicians and domain scientists gain immediately auditable neuroanatomical and molecular networks with exact patient-level risk attributions, while machine learning practitioners gain a flexible family of linear, non-linear decoder, and normalizing-flow alignment modules that preserve both subspace capacity and single-axis disentanglement.
