4.1 Synthesizing Clinical Neurobiology and Statistical Representation Learning

The empirical results across ADNI-MCI, TCGA-BRCA, TCGA-KIRC, and the synthetic ground-truth benchmarks demonstrate that predictive accuracy and mechanistic localization do not need to be traded against one another when multi-view integration is organized around an explicit linear entrypoint (\(\mathbf{Z}_{m,\text{lin}} = \mathbf{X}_m \mathbf{V}_m\)). For the clinical neuroscientist or neuropsychologist, the primary value of SiMR lies in how cross-modal supervision shapes the learned anatomical basis. When structural MRI is decomposed in isolation via PCA, the dominant axis reflects diffuse global cortical thickness and intracranial scaling across all \(62\) DKT parcels (\(0\%\) sparsity), explaining only \(R^2 = 0.152\) of composite cognitive severity (CogPC1) and collapsing to \(R^2 = 0.044\) under single-axis decision tree splits. By coupling structural MRI views to an 11-scale cognitive battery during training (Protocol B) and applying non-negative approximate Stiefel regularization (nsa_flow, \(w = 0.50, q = 0.85\)), the learned projection matrices isolate three non-redundant, \(88\%\)-sparse anatomical systems that mirror classical Braak neurofibrillary staging (Braak and Braak 1991; Dickerson et al. 2009; Jack et al. 2018): the lateral temporoparietal association circuit (Braak IV–V), the entorhinal–hippocampal amnestic core (Braak I–III), and the fronto-insular-cingulate salience network (Seeley et al. 2009; Menon and Uddin 2010), while automatically zeroing out pathologically spared primary motor and somatosensory cortex.

For the machine learning researcher, our four-probe evaluation resolves why post-hoc rotation or unmixing steps (ICA (Hyvärinen and Oja 2000), orthogonal Procrustes rotations (Schönemann 1966), or dense SVD factors) often appear competitive under ridge regression yet fail when individual coordinates are inspected or thresholded. Because \(\operatorname{span}(\mathbf{X}_m \mathbf{V}_m \mathbf{M}) = \operatorname{span}(\mathbf{X}_m \mathbf{V}_m)\) for any invertible \(\mathbf{M} \in \mathrm{GL}(k)\), linear subspace probes measure only aggregate subspace capacity. Pairing linear subspace probes with coordinate-aligned Random Forest probes (RF(XV)) and ground-truth support recovery (\(F_1\)) exposes diagonal coordinate mixing directly: across all twelve benchmarks, nsa_flow-regularized architectures cut the axis-sensitivity drop in half (\(\Delta = -0.022\) vs. \(-0.044\) for PCA) while recovering up to \(84.7\%\) of true pathway supports.

4.3 Limitations and Negative Findings

Rigorous experimental reporting requires highlighting the regimes where SiMR architectures underperform, incur computational trade-offs, or require careful preprocessing:

  1. No Universal Advantage Over PCA on Unaligned Single-View Unsupervised Tasks: When a single homogeneous data matrix is analyzed without cross-modal supervision or when views share identical signal-to-noise ratios and dense Gaussian loadings, standard PCA remains optimal for variance explanation in \(\mathcal{O}(\min(N p^2, p N^2))\) time (\(0.004\text{ s}\)). Similarly, on low-dimensional clinical tables such as Heart (\(p = [7, 6]\)), NSAFlow-PCA (\(0.848\)) slightly exceeds multi-view SiMLR (\(0.831\)) because cross-view graph regularization adds variance when \(p_m\) is very small.
  2. Global Cortical Thickness Confounding Under Unrotated Initialization (varimax = False, q = None): On ADNI-MCI, running unrotated non-negative SiMLR (varimax = False, sparseness = None) leaves Component 1 \(85.5\%\) dense (\(53/62\) active DKT regions, \(15\%\) overall sparsity) because cortical thickness parcels share a positive global correlation floor across the brain (Figure 3.5 A2). In pysimlr v0.2.17, default orthogonal Varimax subspace rotation (varimax = True) resolves this directly in standard column-centered space by rotating the \(k\)-dimensional subspace before non-negative projection, raising unthresholded sparsity from \(15\%\) to \(66\%\) (\([24, 12, 26]\) active parcels, Figure 3.5 A3) without discarding global atrophy severity (unlike manual subject row-mean centering, which yields \(61\%\)–\(68\%\) sparsity but reduces CogPC1 \(R^2\) from \(0.189\) to \(0.153\), as shown in Appendix B). Combining varimax = True with quantile thresholding \(q \in [0.50, 0.85]\) further isolates focal 9-region Braak networks (\(88\%\) sparse, CogPC1 \(R^2 = 0.192\), Figure 3.5 A4).
  3. Stiffness and Accuracy Degradation at Extreme Orthogonality Weights (\(w \ge 0.80\)): Pushing the nsa_flow trade-off weight toward strict Stiefel projection (\(w = 0.95\)) increases wall-clock optimization time by \(2.5\times\)–\(3.8\times\) due to boundary stiffness on the non-negative orthant and reduces ADNI-MCI CogPC1 \(R^2\) from \(0.159\) (\(w = 0.50\)) to \(0.132\) (\(w = 0.95\)). Conversely, setting \(w \le 0.10\) triggers severe rank collapse (\(\text{EffRank} = 2.02\) of \(k = 3\), Gram defect \(D = 0.244\)). The balanced default \(w = 0.50\) avoids both failure modes across all tested cohorts.
  4. Hyperparameter Sensitivity of Deep Multi-View Models on Small Sample Sizes: When sample sizes are moderate (\(N \le 400\) in NonnegParts and Heart), deep autoencoder variants (NED, NEDPP) exhibit higher fold-to-fold variance and lower feature support recovery (\(F_1 = 0.587\)–\(0.642\)) than quasi-Newton linear solvers (SiMLR-LBFGSB, \(F_1 = 0.861\)–\(0.991\)).

4.4 Conclusion

By enforcing an explicit, sparse, non-negative, and approximately orthogonal linear projection at the input boundary of every modality, the SiMR framework bridges classical multivariate biostatistics and modern multi-modal representation learning. Clinicians and domain scientists gain immediately auditable neuroanatomical and molecular networks with exact patient-level risk attributions, while machine learning practitioners gain a flexible family of linear, non-linear decoder, and normalizing-flow alignment modules that preserve both subspace capacity and single-axis disentanglement.
