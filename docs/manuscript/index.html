Abstract

Multi-modal biomedical datasets—such as co-registered neuroimaging, high-throughput omics, and clinical assessments—provide complementary views of shared pathological processes. Integrating these heterogeneous matrices requires dimensionality reduction that isolates cross-view covariance while maintaining interpretable feature attribution. Standard deep multi-view networks and variational autoencoders capture non-linear interactions, but their nested compositions obscure the physical relationship between raw biological measurements and latent coordinates, imposing a severe “transparency penalty” that precludes regulatory adoption in high-stakes clinical decision-making. Conversely, classical linear methods like Canonical Correlation Analysis (CCA) and Principal Component Analysis (PCA) provide auditable projections, but fail to capture non-linear manifold geometry and produce dense, mixed-sign loadings that lack anatomical specificity.

To eliminate this transparency penalty without sacrificing representation capacity, we formulate Deep SiMLR (Similarity-driven Multi-view Reconstruction) deductively across five methodological stages governed by the Only Linear Encoder Entrypoint (OLEE) contract: (1) restricting the input boundary of every modality to a linear projection on the compact Stiefel manifold (\(\mathbf{Z}_{m,\text{lin}} = \mathbf{X}_m \mathbf{V}_m\), \(\mathbf{V}_m^\top \mathbf{V}_m = \mathbf{I}_k\)) with Non-negative Stiefel Approximating Flow (nsa_flow) retraction, guaranteeing globally constant attribution derivatives (\(\nabla_{\mathbf{X}_m} \mathbf{Z}_{m,\text{lin}} = \mathbf{V}_m\)), closed-form counterfactual recourse (\(\delta \mathbf{x}_m^* = \Delta \mathbf{z}_m^* \mathbf{V}_m^\top\)), and zero signed lobe crosstalk (\(\|\mathbf{V}_m^+ \odot \mathbf{V}_m^-\|_1 = 0\)); (2) coupling views via scale-profiled alignment energies, directed/bipartite consensus topologies (topology="graph"), and spectral Modality Alignment Index (MAI) triage; (3) optimizing linear multi-view Stiefel bases via first-order (SiMLR) and quasi-Newton (SiMLR-LBFGS, bound-constrained SiMLR-LBFGSB) Riemannian solvers that provide iteration-0 warm starts; (4) adding non-linear multi-layer perceptron decoding downstream of the linear encoder (LEND) without high-dimensional encoder overfitting (NED, NEDPP); and (5) composing \(\mathbf{V}_m\) with bijective RealNVP normalizing flows (Flow-SiMR-V) trained via detached-prior subspace decoupling (\(\text{sg}(\mathbf{Z}_{m,\text{lin}})\)), which we prove resolves rectangular Stiefel prior collapse onto trailing noise eigenvectors.

We benchmark nine architectures across 324 task evaluations spanning 12 multi-modal data regimes (six synthetic manifolds with known ground truth and six real-world clinical, multi-omics, and imaging cohorts: UCI Cleveland Heart Disease, Efron Diabetes Progression, 3-assay TCGA-BRCA, 3-assay TCGA-KIRC, 4-view ADNI-MCI, and 4-view UCI Multiple Features mfeat) across three independent seeds, alongside a 1,214-combination hyperparameter sweep and a 10-seed mediation benchmark on ADNI-MCI (\(N=1,084\)). Following Demšar’s block-level ranking protocol across the \(B = 12\) datasets, non-parametric inference confirms highly significant architectural separation across both consensus prediction (\(\chi_F^2 = 32.06, p = 9.10 \times 10^{-5}\); Iman–Davenport \(F_F = 5.51, p = 1.19 \times 10^{-5}\); Nemenyi \(CD = 3.468\)) and strictly linear OLEE basis probing (\(\chi_F^2 = 30.77, p = 1.54 \times 10^{-4}\); \(F_F = 5.19, p = 2.54 \times 10^{-5}\)). Hybrid LEND achieves the #1 global rank on consensus prediction (mean rank \(3.58\), mean score \(0.760 \pm 0.249\)) and the highest ground-truth latent recovery (\(CMC = 0.907\)). Bound-constrained quasi-Newton SiMLR-LBFGSB achieves the #1 global rank on the strictly linear OLEE basis probe (mean rank \(3.17\), #1 real-cohort rank \(2.50\), mean \(0.777\)) and the highest sparse support recovery (\(F_1 = 0.991\) vs. PCA \(0.333\)), while detached-prior Flow-SiMR-V ranks #3 overall (\(4.17\) consensus rank, \(4.12\) linear probe rank, #1 real-cohort consensus mean \(0.627\)) and NSAFlow-PCA executes in \(0.040\text{s}\) with \(25.0\%\) disjoint sparsity. Every OLEE-constrained model significantly outperforms unconstrained deep autoencoders (NED, NEDPP, \(p_{\text{Holm}} \le 7.5 \times 10^{-4}\)). In ADNI-MCI (\(N=1,084\)), aligning the three structural brain MRI views (\(382\) features) against the 11 \(>98\%\)-complete cognitive measures via a bipartite training graph (Protocol B) raises MRI-only out-of-sample cognitive factor prediction (CogPC1 R^2) to \(0.2237\) (SiMLR-LBFGSB 3-seed mean) and \(0.2407\) (SiMLR-Lookahead in our 1,214-combination sweep, with detached-prior Flow-SiMR-V reaching \(0.2341\) at \(k=3\) and \(0.2966\) at \(k=6\)) versus \(0.1522\) for PCA, while modeling the two-step cognitive mediation pathway (\(\text{Brain MRI} \to \text{Cognition} \to \text{CDR-SB}\)) boosts out-of-sample prediction of clinical dementia severity over six-fold (\(R^2 = 0.2648\) for LEND-SiMR + MAI; \(R^2 = 0.2443\) in \(0.86\text{s}\) for SiMLR; \(85\text{–}86\%\) indirect effect, \(p < 10^{-15}\)) vs. \(R^2 = 0.0411\) for direct MRI alone.
