This appendix documents the computational environment, replication structure, and non-parametric statistical testing procedures used to generate all tables, figures, and hypothesis tests in the manuscript.

D.1 Building the Manuscript

The manuscript is written in Quarto and compiles to HTML and PDF formats. All benchmark result caches are stored in paper/results_cache/ (deep_ranking_benchmark.csv, operating_points_05_08_benchmark.csv, and adni_mci_1214_grid_sweep.csv), permitting a deterministic build from cached empirical runs:

git clone https://github.com/stnava/pysimlr.git
cd pysimlr
pip install -e ".[dev]"
quarto render paper

D.2 Non-Parametric Benchmark Engine and Optimization Stack

All statistical hypothesis tests, ranking matrices, and permutation null distributions are computed by src/pysimlr/benchmarks/deep_ranking.py:

  • Non-parametric Friedman omnibus test (Friedman 1937) and Iman–Davenport \(F\)-distribution extension (Demšar 2006; Iman and Davenport 1980).
  • Two-tailed Nemenyi Critical Difference (\(CD = 3.468\) at \(\alpha = 0.05\) for \(k = 9, B = 12\)) (Nemenyi 1963; Demšar 2006).
  • Pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank tests (Wilcoxon 1945) with Holm–Bonferroni step-down adjustments (Holm 1979).
  • Quasi-Newton (L-BFGS, L-BFGS-B (Byrd et al. 1995; Nocedal and Wright 2006)) and adaptive first-order (Adam, AdamW (Kingma and Ba 2015; Loshchilov and Hutter 2019)) solvers implemented on top of PyTorch (Paszke et al. 2019), NumPy (Harris et al. 2020), SciPy (Virtanen et al. 2020), and Scikit-Learn (Pedregosa et al. 2011).
  • Benchmark cohorts span clinical cardiology (Heart (Detrano et al. 1989)), metabolic progression (Diabetes (Efron et al. 2004)), handwritten pattern descriptors (mfeat (Breukelen et al. 1998)), transcriptomic/multi-omic cancer benchmarks (Golub et al. 1999; The Cancer Genome Atlas Network 2012; The Cancer Genome Atlas Research Network 2013; Wang et al. 2014; Tenenhaus and Tenenhaus 2011; Tenenhaus et al. 2014; Zhao et al. 2017; Abdi et al. 2013), and Alzheimer’s neuroimaging (ADNI-MCI (Mueller et al. 2005; Jack et al. 2008; Weiner et al. 2017)).

The test suite tests/test_deep_ranking_stats.py verifies the statistical calculations:

pytest tests/test_deep_ranking_stats.py -v

D.3 Replication Structure and the Permutation Resolution Floor

D.3.1 Block-Level Sign-Flip Permutation Floor

In paired comparisons across \(B = 12\) independent dataset blocks (where each block cell is the mean across \(N_{\text{seeds}} = 3\) replicates (Demšar 2006)), the exact distribution-free sign-flip null distribution across the \(B = 12\) blocks consists of \(2^B = 4,096\) sign assignments:

\[ p_{\text{perm}} = \frac{1}{2^B} \sum_{\mathbf{s} \in \{-1, +1\}^B} \mathbb{I}\left(\left|\frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B s_i \Delta_i\right| \ge |\bar{\Delta}|\right), \qquad p_{\min, B=12} = \frac{2}{2^{12}} = \frac{2}{4096} \approx 0.000488 \]
