B.1 Effect of the Approximate Stiefel Weight \(w \in [0.01, 0.95]\)

Table B.1 and Figure B.1 evaluate SiMLR-LBFGSB (\(\mathbf{V}_m \ge 0, k = 3\)) across ten values of \(w \in [0.01, 0.95]\) on synthetic NonnegParts and clinical ADNI-MCI (Protocol B).

B.2 Preprocessing Controls: Subject Row-Mean Removal and ICA Initialization

On neuroimaging cohorts where cortical regions share a positive global correlation floor, Table B.2 and Figure B.2 compare standard column centering against in-fold subject row-mean centering, deterministic FastICA (Hyvärinen and Oja 2000) initialization, and latent rank scaling \(k \in \{2, 3, 5, 8, 12\}\).

B.3 Fine-Grained Quantile Sparsity (\(q \in [\text{None}, 0.90]\)) and ADNI-MCI 1,214-Sweep Summary

Table B.3 reports the complete quantile sparseness progression from \(q = \text{None}\) to \(q = 0.90\) on ADNI-MCI and NonnegParts, and Table B.4 reports the top configuration per model family across the \(1,214\)-configuration ADNI-MCI grid sweep.

B.4 Synthetic Per-Regime Breakdown and Pairwise Wilcoxon Tests

Table B.5 and Figure B.3 report the 3-seed performance across each of the six synthetic ground-truth regimes, and Table B.6 reports pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank tests against PCA across the \(N = 36\) dataset-seed pairs.
